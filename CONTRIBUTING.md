# Contributing

Add anything that helps someone pick, run or trust a decision model.

## What belongs

- **Models:** any non-generative model returning typed choice/score/probability outputs. Say the size and license, and link the weights or API.
- **Benchmarks:** say who ran it, on what hardware and data, and whether it was replicated.
- **Papers:** arXiv or equivalent. Give a 2–3 sentence summary with the key numbers.
- **Tools:** must be linkable and have a README. Put it in the closest category.

## Entry rules

1. One line per entry: `[name](url)`, then what decision it makes.
2. **Say what you verified.** "Ran it," "read the README," and "listed elsewhere" are different claims. Don't upgrade one to another.
3. Report numbers as their authors reported them, and name the author. If two sources disagree, show both.
4. Don't include marketing claims without a measurement behind them.
5. New category? Open an issue first if it would have fewer than 3 entries.
